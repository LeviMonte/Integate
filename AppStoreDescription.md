## Description

Stop scrolling. Start solving.

MathGate is a screen time app with a twist — you don't just set limits, you *earn* your time back by solving real math problems. Solve a problem, unlock minutes. The harder the problem, the more time you earn.

**Five subjects. Four difficulty levels. No shortcuts.**

Choose from Integrals, Derivatives, Algebra, SAT Math, or AP Physics — then pick your level from Basic through Expert. Every problem must be solved digit by digit, so you can't just guess.

**Built to actually teach you**

The Learn tab isn't just a formula sheet. Every topic comes with:
• A plain-English explanation of *why* the math works — not just how
• Worked examples with full step-by-step solutions
• Common mistakes to avoid
• Links to Khan Academy and 3Blue1Brown videos
• Practice problems you can expand and check

**Earn time, build streaks**

Correct answers add real minutes to your screen time window. First-try solves earn more. Use a hint and take a time penalty. Fail a problem and get the full worked solution so you learn from it.

**Track your progress**

Watch your XP climb, your streak grow, and your accuracy improve across every subject. See exactly which levels you've unlocked and how many problems you've solved.

**Honest about how it works**

MathGate uses an honor system — it notifies you when your earned time runs out, and the commitment to actually close those apps is yours. Real app blocking requires an Apple Family Controls entitlement we're working toward.

Whether you're prepping for the SAT, studying for AP Calculus, or just want to feel like you *earned* that TikTok session — MathGate makes screen time mean something.
