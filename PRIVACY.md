# Integate Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 2026**

## Data Collection

Integate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information — including your progress, streaks, XP, solved problems, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults and is never sent to any server.

## Third-Party Services

Integate does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
