# MathGate Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 2026**

MathGate ("the app") is developed by Levi Monte.

## Data Collection

MathGate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information — including your progress, streaks, XP, solved problems, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults and is never sent to any server.

## Third-Party Services

MathGate does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
